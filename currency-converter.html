Currency converter for MTBank

Designed an in-app currency exchange feature that allows customers to convert money online 24/7 at competitive rates without visiting a bank branch

Overview

Project: Moby is the mobile banking application of MTBank.

MTBank is a top-10 Belarusian bank providing technological financial services for private clients and businesses since 1994.

September 2023 - till now

Mobile app design, Material Design, Human Interface Guidelines (HIG), UX and Visual Design, Research, Competitive analysis, Prototyping, Wireframing, Design Systems

Challenge

Problem

Customers must visit physical bank branches to exchange currency, a time-consuming process that requires planning and waiting in line. This results in poor user experience, increased branch load and customer churn to competitors offering digital solutions.

Objective

Implement currency exchange in the Moby app through Nembo service integration. Customers can exchange currency 24/7 at competitive rates without visiting a branch, while the bank avoids significant ABS modifications thanks to Nembo's existing banking system integration.

[ 01 ]

Design journey

[ a ] Research & Analysis

This stage consisted of two key components:

  • Researched existing currency exchange flows in banking apps and analyzed competitor solutions to identify best practices and common pain points.
  • Investigate how this functionality could be implemented for Android.
[ b ] Design & Iteration

Conducted multiple design iterations incorporating feedback from the client, product manager, and developers to achieve optimal usability and compliance with requirements.

[ c ] Collaboration & Validation

Collaborated with developers on Nembo integration, conducted design reviews to ensure visual consistency, and worked with the QA team to validate the implementation before launch.

[ 02 ] User Scenarios & States Mapping

When making a purchase, the order goes through specific statuses within the app, and all of these statuses have been implemented in Live Activities:

A unique visual element was designed for each status:

1. Icon (see Chapter 4)

2. Heading

3. Text message (e.g. "Your order is on the way")

4. Progress bar (helps to determine the order's status in a few seconds)

[ 03 ]

Results & Impact

Business Outcomes:
  • Faster Time-to-Market: launched in 2 months vs 6+ months it would take to build a custom exchange module from scratch.
  • Cost Efficiency: no major ABS rework required, reducing development costs.
  • Enhanced Competitiveness: added in-demand currency exchange functionality to strengthen the app's market position.
User Experience:
  • 24/7 Availability: currency exchange accessible anytime without branch visits.
  • Faster Transactions: reduced exchange time from 15-30 minutes in branch to just 1-3 minutes in-app.
  • Rate Benefits:options to improve rates via amount increase or promo codes.
